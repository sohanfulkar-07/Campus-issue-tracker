ABOUT THE PROJECT

Empowering Campus Operations Through
Tech-Driven Accountability

CampusTracker was born out of a critical academic necessity: to bridge the operational gap between students, faculty members, and structural administrators. Traditional grievance systems rely heavily on manual tracking, leading to delayed resolution speeds, misplaced requests, and complete lack of visibility into system bottlenecks.

Our goal is to build an uncompromised framework where every infrastructure breakdown, academic support ticket, or maintenance emergency is instantly captured, intelligently sorted, and actively monitored until resolution—fostering absolute transparency across the entire institute.
